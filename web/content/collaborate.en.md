[PLACEHOLDER] Sardigna Atlas shows Sardinia through the people and things that stayed for life. We work with food creators and brands that fit that story.

## For food creators

A creator is a **guest author** with us: you get your own profile (photo, bio, links, a note about your collaborations) and bylined pieces. The editors enter the content, so you need no account or editing tools. Scope, copyright, photo licensing and any fee are agreed in a contract [PLACEHOLDER].

[Write to us as a creator](/en/contact/?topic=creator)

## For brands

We consider working with food and kitchen-equipment brands when the product relates to the site's subject. Kinds of collaboration:

- **affiliate links**: plain links, always with a visible label,
- **sponsored content**: labelled as advertising,
- **paid collaboration**: a piece or profile made together with a brand, labelled,
- **products provided free of charge**: labelled as such.

[Write to us as a brand](/en/contact/?topic=brand)

## Rules

- **We always label, at the top of the piece.** Readers can see whether a piece contains affiliate links, is sponsored, was made in a collaboration or with products from the maker.
- **Editorial independence.** Paid collaboration does not change our criteria or guarantee a positive opinion. [PLACEHOLDER] Wording for approval.
- **Plain links only.** No embedded widgets, price search boxes or partner iframes.

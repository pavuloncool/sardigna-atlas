# Cookie policy

> [PLACEHOLDER] Draft to be reviewed by a lawyer familiar with Polish and EU law before commercial launch.

Version 1.0 of the site **sets no cookies** and shows no consent banner. Your chosen theme (light/dark) is stored in your browser's `localStorage`, only on your own device; it is never sent to a server.

## Future changes

If we later enable services that require consent (for example advertising or cookie-based analytics), we will ask for your consent before loading them, and this page will be updated with the list of services, consent categories, cookie names and retention periods.
